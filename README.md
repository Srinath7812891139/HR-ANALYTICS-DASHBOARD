# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to identify key factors influencing employee attrition, workforce distribution, job roles, departments, and employee demographics. Using Tableau, the project applies data analysis, visualization, calculated fields, KPIs, filters, and interactive dashboard design to uncover meaningful HR insights.

The primary objective is to help organizations understand workforce patterns, identify areas of high employee attrition, and support data-driven human resource decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze employee demographics and workforce characteristics.
* Identify key factors influencing employee attrition.
* Analyze employee distribution across departments and job roles.
* Create an interactive Tableau dashboard for HR insights.
* Measure important HR metrics using KPIs.
* Provide actionable recommendations to improve employee retention.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Employee attrition varies across different departments and job roles.
* Employees working overtime may show higher attrition levels.
* Age and years of experience can influence employee retention patterns.
* Salary and job satisfaction provide useful indicators for understanding employee behavior.
* Employee segmentation helps HR teams identify groups that may require additional retention strategies.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition and develop targeted retention strategies.
* Monitor employees who frequently work overtime and improve workload management.
* Improve employee satisfaction through career development and engagement programs.
* Analyze compensation patterns to identify potential salary-related retention issues.
* Use employee segmentation to develop personalized HR strategies.
* Regularly monitor attrition KPIs through interactive dashboards.
